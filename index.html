    How the indicators are calculated

    Expected reporting days — every date column in the IHIP export is one expected report for each listed facility (Sundays and holidays without a column are not counted). Cell values: Y reported with cases, NIL reported nil, N not reported.

    Reporting completeness % = (Y + NIL days) ÷ expected reporting days × 100, counted cell by cell from the daily columns, never from pre-aggregated totals.

    NIL share = NIL days ÷ reported days. A rising NIL share with steady completeness is a data-quality signal worth checking at the facility (zero-reporting fatigue).

    Performance band for each facility over the selected period: Regular ≥ target (80%), Irregular 50% to below target, Poor below 50%, Silent 0% (no report on any expected day).

    Longest gap = most consecutive expected reporting days not reported. Current gap = consecutive non-reported days up to the last date in the period, the first list for phone follow-up.

    Facilities meeting target = facilities with completeness ≥ target ÷ facilities with at least one expected day. Forms are never pooled: S, P and L have different reporting units, so each is analysed on its own and compared side by side.

    Not available in the export: report timeliness (time of submission) and case counts. Completeness here measures whether a facility reported on each day, not when.
